LZY Health Tracker

About This Application

LZY Health Tracker is a personal health and fitness data management application.

The application integrates with the Oura API to retrieve personal sleep, heart rate, activity, and recovery data.

Purpose

The application is designed for private, non-commercial health and fitness tracking.

Health data may be securely synchronized to the account owner's private Google Drive for personal analysis.
